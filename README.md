## My favorite movie

1. La la land

**La La Land** is a critically acclaimed 2016 **American musical romantic drama** film written and directed by Damien Chazelle. The movie stars Ryan Gosling and Emma Stone as a struggling jazz pianist and an aspiring actress who fall in love while pursuing their Hollywood dreams in Los Angeles. The film is celebrated for its sweeping musical numbers, vibrant cinematography, and bittersweet portrayal of ambition versus romance.

2. Fight club

**Fight Club** is a **dark, psychological cult** classic film released in 1999, directed by David Fincher and based on the 1996 novel by Chuck Palahniuk. The movie stars Edward Norton as an unnamed, insomniac office worker and Brad Pitt as Tyler Durden, a charismatic and anti-consumerist soap salesman. Together, they form an underground fight club as a radical form of therapy, which quickly spirals into a dangerous, anti-corporate extremist movement known as Project Mayhem.
